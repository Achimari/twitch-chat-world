Activities

Automatic UFO escapes run only while Stream session active is on (it switches off when the server restarts) or demo mode is on. They wait for the gap after the previous activity, enough recent Twitch chatters (people who chatted and still have a character; demo and test-chat users don't count unless demo mode is on), and a free slot. If nobody joins, the next gap grows (up to 4×). One big event runs at a time: buttons, voice triggers and !party are rejected while another runs, never queued. Automatic activities alternate between UFO escape and the goofy race (if race prediction is on). Play coins are free and session-only: 100 per viewer, no purchases or real value. They reset when you switch the stream session on, change channel or restart the server, never on an OBS refresh.

Sound

OBS plays the sound. The preview here is silent unless you turn on Preview sound (a click here does not unlock OBS; they are separate browsers). In OBS, tick Control audio via OBS on the Browser Source and check its meter while pressing Test sound. Only one source should make sound: add ?silent=1 to any extra overlay URL.
